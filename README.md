# 🎓 StudXWork - Student Hustle Hub

A modern full-stack platform designed to connect students and recruiters in one place.

StudXWork enables students to discover job opportunities, complete tasks, access study resources, communicate with recruiters, and manage their earnings through an integrated wallet system. The platform provides a seamless and responsive experience across all devices.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student Panel

* User Registration & Login
* Browse Jobs & Tasks
* Apply for Jobs & Tasks
* Access Study Notes & Resources
* Real-time Messaging System
* Wallet Management
* Request & Application Tracking
* View Notices & Announcements
* Contact Support Team
* Manage Profile & Account Settings

### 🏢 Recruiter Panel

* Recruiter Registration & Login
* Post Jobs & Tasks
* Manage Applications
* Communicate with Students
* Track Posted Opportunities

### 🛠️ Admin Panel

* Secure Admin Authentication
* Dashboard & Analytics
* Manage Students & Recruiters
* Manage Jobs & Tasks
* Manage Applications & Requests
* Handle Support Tickets
* Manage Notices & Announcements
* Monitor Wallet Transactions
* Platform Content Management
